## Prompt

```
For my vacation rental business, Susan acts as the property manager. Susan has been working for me for about a year. James will be a new hire acting as my as a personal assistant. His primary responsibilities will include managing rental-property repair work, including hiring/supervising repair sub-contractors. 

The issue I'm having is that Susan feels threatened because there is overlap between her responsibilities and James's. For example, hiring and supervising repair people. Please suggest a list of seven different strategies I could use to minimize the potential friction between Susan and James.
```
